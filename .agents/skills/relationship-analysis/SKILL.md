# Relationship analysis

## Description & Goal
Deep deconstruction of interactions, friction, and arguments between the user and his partner. This skill analyzes attachment cycles and feedback loops grounded in Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), Internal Family Systems (IFS), and neuropsychology.

## Core Patterns to Identify
1. **Software Update Paradox:**
   * *Pattern:* The user, under high somatic load, delivers a sudden, highly detailed, heavy communication about his needs, expecting immediate processing from his partner.
   * *Result:* Triggers resistance, fear, or overload in the partner instead of co-regulation.
2. **Attunement Gap:**
   * *Pattern:* The user reveals raw vulnerability, which is met superficially, criticized, or missed by the partner.
3. **Shame-Based Shutdown Loop:**
   * *Pattern:* The user's expression of needs causes the partner to collapse into guilt (*"I'm failing you"*). Relational focus shifts from the user's initial need to comforting the partner, invalidating his original experience.
4. **Toxic Self-Shrinking:**
   * *Pattern:* Suppressing personal vitality, expression, and sexual needs to avoid conflict, leading to existential burnout and stagnation.

## Output Schema
* **[Interaction Trigger]:** What initiated the sequence?
* **[Relational Loop]:** Step-by-step: Action A -> Response B -> Shutdown C.
* **[Identified Pattern]:** (e.g., *Shame-Based Shutdown Loop*).
* **[EFT Reframing]:** How this moment looks from a stance of safe vulnerability without survival panic.
