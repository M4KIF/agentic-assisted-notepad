# Nervous-system overload analysis

## Description & Goal
Quantitative and qualitative assessment of Allostatic Overload, vagal capacity, and the user's active window of tolerance based on notes and behavioral cues.

## Assessment Matrix

| Indicator | Low Risk (In-Window) | Overload (Hyper-arousal / Freeze) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Head State** | Clarity, cognitive flexibility | "Hot anvil", mental chatter, decision paralysis |
| **Body / Stomach** | Calm, diaphragmatic breath | Stomach clenching, tightness, somatic freeze |
| **Language** | Needs-focused, felt-sense ("I feel") | Heavy intellectualization, clinical terms |
| **Interaction** | Open to touch and listening | Threat scanning, withdrawal, dopamine rushes |

## Analytical Directive
1. Rate overload on a scale of 1–10.
2. When the score is $\ge 7/10$: **HALT** all theoretical analysis and decision-making. Pivot the interaction exclusively to somatic recovery protocols (Somatic Tracking, deep exhalations, grounding, taking a communication break until ventral vagal tone returns).
